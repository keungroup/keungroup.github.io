01 / About

A factory, kept in the family.

Keun isn't built on a vacant lot. It's a working compound — held by one family for four generations — being renovated in place.

The original buildings went up as a textile factory in the 1950s. The brick walls, the sawtooth roofs, the loading bays opening onto an interior courtyard — none of it can be sourced from a catalog, and none of it can be replicated on a new-build site.

Every other wellness destination in Bandung is starting from a leased shell. Keun is starting from a story. That's the part no competitor can copy: a piece of the city's industrial history, kept, restored, and re-programmed for how people actually want to spend a day in 2026.
