01 / About

A place with a past. A vision for what's next.

Keun is not built on a blank canvas.

For more than four generations, this site has remained in the care of one family. What began as a textile mill in the 1950s is now being carefully restored and reimagined as a contemporary destination for movement, recovery, work, and connection.

The architecture remains deeply rooted in its origins. Brick walls shaped by decades of use. Sawtooth roofs that once brought daylight into factory floors. Loading bays opening onto a central courtyard that now forms the heart of the experience.

While many wellness destinations begin within leased commercial spaces, Keun begins with something far rarer: a sense of permanence. A site with history, character, and a legacy that extends beyond a business plan.

Rather than erase the past, Keun builds upon it to preserve a piece of Bandung's industrial heritage and transform it into a destination designed for how people want to live today.

02 / The Day

A day at Keun.

Everything you need, in one place.

Move, recover, work, dine, and reconnect without crossing the city. Every space is designed to flow naturally into the next, allowing an entire day to unfold at your own pace.

Start the morning with reformer Pilates, hot mat, or an energizing spin session.

08:15 — 10:00
Slow down over coffee.

Settle into the courtyard with breakfast, a morning coffee, or a few uninterrupted hours of focused work.

10:30

Head spa treatments, cuts, and colour services designed around care rather than rush.

13:00 — 14:00
Lunch, without leaving.

A midday pause shared over good food, natural light, and familiar faces.

15:00
Recover and reset.

Deep tissue massage and bodywork to release tension and restore balance.

Contrast therapy designed to support recovery, circulation, and mental clarity.

19:30 — Late

Dinner, conversation, and an atmosphere that invites you to stay a little longer.

03 / Floorplan

Two floors. One continuous rhythm.

Keun is structured around how a day unfolds. Public life at ground level. Movement and recovery above. Each floor transitions naturally into the next, removing friction between arrival, activity, and rest.
